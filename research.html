Research

My work lies at the interface of semiclassical analysis, microlocal analysis and quantum dynamics, with a particular focus on magnetic Schrödinger operators.

01

Semiclassical analysis

Semiclassical measures, high-frequency asymptotics and long-time propagation for quantum systems.

02

Magnetic Schrödinger operators

Quantum dynamics in the presence of magnetic fields, with emphasis on semiclassical limits and invariant measures.

03

Microlocal dynamics

Microlocal and two-microlocal techniques for resonant dynamics, equidistribution and spectral questions.

Current themes

Magnetic perturbations, semiclassical measures on the torus, long-time Schrödinger dynamics, resonances and spectral asymptotics.
