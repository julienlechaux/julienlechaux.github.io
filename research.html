Research

My research lies at the interface of semiclassical analysis, microlocal analysis and quantum dynamics, with a particular interest in magnetic Schrödinger operators.

Current themes

Magnetic perturbations, semiclassical measures on the torus, long-time Schrödinger dynamics, resonances and spectral asymptotics.
