Julien Lechaux

PhD student in Mathematics

Laboratoire de Mathématiques Jean Leray

I am a PhD student in mathematics at Nantes Université, under the supervision of Gabriel Rivière . My research lies at the interface of semiclassical analysis, microlocal analysis and quantum dynamics, with a particular interest in magnetic Schrödinger operators, semiclassical measures, long-time dynamics and spectral theory.
